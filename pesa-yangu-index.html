FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is my M-Pesa data safe? ⌄
Yes, completely. Pesa Yangu does NOT connect to your M-Pesa account. You manually enter transactions. All your data is stored locally on your device — we never see it, never store it on our servers, and never sell it.
How do I pay for Premium? ⌄
You pay KES 199/month via M-Pesa. After clicking "Pay with M-Pesa", enter your phone number and you will receive an STK Push prompt on your phone. Confirm the payment and Premium is activated instantly.
Can I cancel Premium anytime? ⌄
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You keep Premium access until the end of your paid month. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
Do I need to download an app? ⌄
No! Pesa Yangu works directly in your mobile browser. Open pesayangu.co.ke on Chrome or Safari, and you can even add it to your home screen so it feels like an app — with zero storage used.
Does it work without internet? ⌄
Yes! Once loaded, the app works fully offline. Your data is saved on your device. You only need internet to initially load the app or to upgrade to Premium.
Is it only for M-Pesa? ⌄
No — you can track any transaction: cash, bank, Equity, KCB, Airtel Money, or anything else. We're designed for M-Pesa first but work for all Kenyan money movements.
